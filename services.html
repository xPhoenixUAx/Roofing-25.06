How we decide scope

Repair when possible. Replace when responsible.

The goal is not to sell the largest job. The goal is to identify what will stop water, preserve structure, satisfy code, and make financial sense over the next several seasons.

A better request usually gets a better first conversation.

PhotosShow leaks, missing shingles, gutters, attic stains, or storm damage.
TimingSay whether it is urgent, this week, this month, or planning ahead.
Property notesMention home, rental, business, roof access, steep pitch, or low-slope areas.
