Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

RoofLink Roofing Network collects information that visitors voluntarily submit through request forms, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, locations, service interests, property types, urgency, and project details.

How information is used

Information is used to route roofing requests, communicate with visitors, support provider matching, and maintain records related to submitted inquiries.

How information is protected

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to limit access to inquiry details. We do not sell submitted contact information.

Third-party services

If future tools such as analytics, maps, call tracking, or scheduling platforms are added, they should be configured only with IDs supplied by the site owner.
