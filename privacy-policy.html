Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026. RoofLink Roofing Network is a free roofing request and provider-matching website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when a visitor submits a roofing request, contacts the site, uses a form, clicks a phone or email link, or browses pages about roof repair, replacement, inspections, storm damage, gutters, drainage, and commercial roofing provider categories.

RoofLink is not a roofing contractor and does not perform roofing work. The purpose of collecting request information is to help organize roofing needs and connect visitors with independent local providers who may be able to respond. Any provider that contacts a visitor is independent from RoofLink unless clearly stated otherwise.

Information visitors provide

Visitors may voluntarily submit personal and project information through request forms, contact forms, calls, emails, or similar communication tools. The exact fields may vary by page, but submitted information may include:

  • Name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, and other contact details.
  • Property location details such as city, ZIP code, address, service area, or general roof location.
  • Roofing category, property type, urgency, roof age, roof type, visible damage, leak location, storm date, gutter symptoms, commercial building details, and other request notes.
  • Photos, attachments, written descriptions, or other details a visitor chooses to provide to explain the roofing need.
  • Communication records, including messages sent through forms, email replies, call notes, appointment preferences, and follow-up requests.

Information collected automatically

When visitors use the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website host, browser, security tools, analytics tools, form tools, or future third-party services configured by the site owner. This may include IP address, device type, browser type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from technical data, timestamps, error logs, form submission metadata, and interaction data such as clicks or page navigation.

This information helps operate the website, diagnose technical issues, understand which pages are useful, prevent spam, and improve the request experience. It is not intended to determine whether a visitor should hire any particular provider.

How information is used

RoofLink may use collected information for practical website and request-routing purposes, including:

  • Receiving, organizing, and reviewing roofing requests submitted by homeowners, property managers, or business contacts.
  • Routing a request to independent local providers or provider networks that may handle the selected roofing category and service area.
  • Communicating with visitors about a submitted request, missing information, follow-up, support questions, or form confirmation.
  • Helping providers understand request category, urgency, location, property type, and basic project details before contacting the visitor.
  • Maintaining internal records related to submitted inquiries, provider routing, spam prevention, quality review, and website performance.
  • Improving page content, forms, category descriptions, user experience, and the clarity of the request process.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or recordkeeping obligations where applicable.

How information may be shared

Because RoofLink is an aggregator and request-routing website, submitted request information may be shared with independent roofing providers, lead-routing partners, call centers, software vendors, hosting providers, form processors, email tools, analytics providers, security services, or other operational vendors that support the website and provider-matching process.

When request information is shared with an independent provider, that provider may use the information to contact the visitor, ask follow-up questions, schedule an inspection, discuss timing, provide an estimate, or decide whether the request fits their services. Provider use of information may be governed by that provider's own privacy practices. Visitors should ask any provider how their information will be used if they have concerns before continuing the conversation.

RoofLink does not sell submitted contact information as a consumer data product. However, because the website exists to connect visitors with independent providers, request information may be distributed or transferred as part of lead routing, provider matching, operational support, or business administration.

Independent providers

Providers are independent businesses or professionals. RoofLink does not control provider licensing, insurance, workmanship, pricing, scheduling, warranties, response times, or privacy practices. Visitors are responsible for deciding whether to speak with, schedule, or hire any provider. Before hiring, visitors should verify license, insurance, references, written scope, pricing, warranty terms, and any contract terms directly with the provider.

Calls, texts, and email communications

By submitting contact information, a visitor authorizes RoofLink, its operational partners, and matched independent providers to contact the visitor about the submitted roofing request. Contact may occur by phone, text message, email, voicemail, or similar communication methods, depending on the information supplied and tools in use.

Message and data rates may apply for texts or mobile communications. Visitors can ask to stop receiving non-essential communications, but request-related or administrative communications may still be sent when necessary to process a request, respond to an inquiry, or maintain records.

Data retention

RoofLink may retain submitted request information for as long as reasonably necessary to route requests, maintain business records, evaluate provider matching, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and improve website operations. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information, operational needs, legal requirements, and whether a request resulted in provider follow-up.

Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards may be used to protect submitted information. No website, form, email system, phone system, hosting provider, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Visitors should avoid submitting highly sensitive information that is not necessary to describe a roofing request.

Visitor choices

Visitors may choose not to submit a request form. Visitors may also contact RoofLink to ask about submitted information, request correction of inaccurate contact details, ask to stop certain communications, or request deletion where applicable and technically feasible. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, recordkeeping, dispute-resolution, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking roofing provider information for homes, rental properties, managed properties, or commercial buildings. It is not intended for children, and RoofLink does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Third-party websites and tools

The website may link to third-party websites, maps, call tools, provider websites, analytics tools, or other services. RoofLink is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party services. Visitors should review the privacy notices of any third-party website or provider they choose to use.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, provider-routing practices, technology tools, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the website after an update means the visitor has had an opportunity to review the updated policy.
