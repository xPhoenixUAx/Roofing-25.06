Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

IronPeak Roofing Co. collects information that visitors voluntarily submit through contact and estimate forms, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, property addresses, service interests, and project details.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to roofing inquiries, schedule inspections, prepare estimates, communicate project details, and maintain records related to requested services.

How information is protected

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to limit access to inquiry details. We do not sell submitted contact information.

Third-party services

If future tools such as analytics, maps, call tracking, or scheduling platforms are added, they should be configured only with IDs supplied by the site owner.
