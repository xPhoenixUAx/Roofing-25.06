Cookie Policy

Home/Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026. This Cookie Policy explains how cookies, pixels, local storage, scripts, tags, log files, and similar technologies may be used on the RoofLink Roofing Network website. The website is a roofing request and provider-matching resource, so some technology may be used to keep forms working, understand site performance, prevent spam, and support request routing.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser when a visitor uses a website. Similar technologies may store information in browser local storage, session storage, server logs, analytics events, call-tracking systems, form tools, or security tools. These technologies can help a website remember preferences, measure page activity, support forms, and protect against abuse.

How RoofLink may use cookies and similar technologies

RoofLink may use cookies or similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation: supporting page loading, navigation, form submission, mobile menu behavior, spam prevention, security checks, and basic website functionality.
  • Form and request support: helping contact forms operate, preserving request context, reducing duplicate submissions, and supporting confirmation messages.
  • Analytics and performance: understanding which pages visitors use, how forms perform, what service categories are viewed, and where technical errors occur.
  • Communication tools: supporting phone links, email links, call tracking, scheduling tools, or similar systems if they are added by the site owner.
  • Advertising or measurement: measuring campaign performance or routing traffic sources if advertising pixels, conversion tags, or remarketing tools are intentionally configured in the future.

Current website configuration

The website is designed as a static provider-matching site and does not require visitors to create accounts. Some browser or server-side data may still be generated by hosting, security, form handling, embedded scripts, or standard browser behavior. If analytics, call tracking, maps, chat, scheduling, advertising pixels, or other third-party tools are added later, those tools may place their own cookies or collect similar technical data.

Types of cookies that may be used

Strictly necessary cookies

These support basic website operation and security. They may be needed for forms, navigation, load balancing, bot prevention, or remembering a session long enough to complete a request. Disabling them may cause parts of the website to stop working correctly.

Preference cookies

Preference technologies may remember choices such as form state, region, service category, cookie settings, or interface preferences. These are used to make the website easier to use and reduce repetitive input where supported.

Analytics cookies

Analytics technologies may help measure page views, service-category interest, form starts, form submissions, device types, browser issues, referral sources, and general user flow. This information helps improve website content and request clarity. Analytics data may be aggregated or pseudonymous depending on the tool used.

Advertising and conversion cookies

If advertising tools are added, cookies or pixels may help measure whether a visitor submitted a roofing request after clicking an ad or visiting a campaign page. These tools may also help limit repetitive ads or understand marketing performance. RoofLink should only use these tools when configured by the site owner with appropriate notice and consent settings where required.

Third-party cookies

Third-party providers may set cookies when their tools are embedded or connected to the website. Examples may include hosting platforms, analytics services, form processors, security tools, call-tracking services, email platforms, map tools, video tools, scheduling tools, advertising networks, or provider-routing platforms. Third-party cookies are controlled by the third party, and their use may be governed by that party's own policy.

Provider matching and cookie data

Cookie data is not a substitute for the details a visitor submits in a roofing request. Provider matching depends primarily on request information such as service category, location, urgency, property type, and project description. Technical data may help operate forms or understand website performance, but it does not guarantee provider response, pricing, availability, or project acceptance.

Managing cookies

Visitors can control cookies through browser settings. Most browsers allow visitors to block cookies, delete existing cookies, limit third-party cookies, or receive warnings before cookies are stored. Browser controls vary by device and browser. Blocking cookies may affect form behavior, security tools, analytics accuracy, or embedded third-party features.

Visitors may also use privacy tools, device settings, advertising preferences, or third-party opt-out tools where available. If a cookie consent tool is added to the website, visitors should use that tool to manage applicable categories when presented.

Do Not Track and privacy signals

Some browsers send Do Not Track or similar signals. Because there is not one uniform industry standard for responding to every signal, website behavior may vary based on the tools configured. Where legally required or technically supported, the website or connected tools may respond to recognized consent choices, opt-out preferences, or privacy signals.

Retention

Cookies may last only during a browsing session or remain for a longer period depending on their purpose and the tool setting them. Server logs and analytics records may be retained separately from browser cookies for security, performance, operational, or reporting purposes.

Updates to this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if the website adds or changes analytics, form tools, tracking tags, advertising pixels, call tracking, embedded media, or other technologies. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date.
