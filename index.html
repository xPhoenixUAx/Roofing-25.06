Before you hire

Use provider replies to ask better questions.

A good match is not only about who answers first. Compare what each provider says about the problem, timeline, inspection, materials, warranty, and payment terms.

Verify credentials

Confirm license and insurance requirements for the work being performed.

Ask for written scope

Request clear notes on labor, materials, cleanup, timing, and exclusions.

Watch urgent damage

Active leaks, exposed decking, and storm openings may need faster follow-up.

Compare communication

Choose the provider who explains the work clearly and answers your questions.
