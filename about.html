Our approach

We look beyond the obvious leak

Roof problems rarely start in one isolated spot. A stain on the ceiling may connect to a cracked pipe boot, an overdriven nail, clogged gutters, missing kick-out flashing, poor attic ventilation, or a previous repair that never addressed the source.

IronPeak Roofing Co. was built around a complete-cycle method: inspect, explain, stabilize, repair or replace, and maintain. That approach helps homeowners and property managers spend money where it solves the real issue.
