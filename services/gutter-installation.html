Roof protection continues at the gutter line

Gutters are part of the roof system. When they sag, clog, overflow, or drain too close to the foundation, water can damage fascia, soffits, siding, landscaping, basements, and the roof edge itself.

RoofLink helps users describe roof-edge and drainage issues so independent providers can discuss gutter repair or replacement needs.

Included gutter services

  • Seamless gutter installation and replacement for residential rooflines.
  • Downspout placement, extension planning, splash control, and water routing away from foundations.
  • Gutter slope correction, hanger replacement, leak sealing, and end-cap repair.
  • Fascia and drip edge review where gutter failure has allowed water to back up.
  • Gutter guard conversations based on trees, debris load, roof pitch, and maintenance expectations.
  • Seasonal cleaning and roof-edge maintenance recommendations.

Why drainage matters

Good shingles cannot compensate for water that repeatedly backs up under the edge, spills behind gutters, or saturates nearby materials. Drainage service helps preserve the roof replacement investment and reduces avoidable exterior damage.

Best for

Overflowing gutters, rotted fascia, poor downspout routing, roof-edge stains, basement water concerns, and seasonal maintenance.
