Full roof rebuild

A complete system, not just new shingles

A roof replacement should correct the weaknesses that caused premature wear, not simply cover them with new materials. IronPeak plans replacement around decking condition, ventilation balance, water-shedding paths, roof edges, flashing details, and the way storms hit the property.

The process begins with inspection and measurement, then moves into material selection, schedule planning, protection of landscaping and exterior surfaces, tear-off, installation, cleanup, and final walkthrough.

What is included

  • Removal and disposal of old roofing materials down to the agreed substrate.
  • Decking review with clear communication before any required wood replacement.
  • Synthetic underlayment, ice and water protection where appropriate, drip edge, starter strip, field shingles, hip and ridge materials.
  • Replacement or resetting of vents, pipe boots, step flashing, counter flashing, and transition details.
  • Ventilation review to reduce heat buildup, condensation risk, and uneven shingle aging.
  • Magnetic nail sweep, debris removal, final photo review, and care guidance.

Material guidance

We explain differences between architectural shingles, impact-resistant options, metal details, underlayment upgrades, ridge ventilation, and color choices. The recommendation is based on slope, exposure, budget, neighborhood requirements, and expected property ownership timeline.

Best for

End-of-life roofs, recurring leaks, widespread granule loss, brittle shingles, decking concerns, and major storm damage.
