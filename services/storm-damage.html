Wind and hail response

Stabilize damage, document findings, restore the roof

After hail, high wind, heavy snow, or flying debris, the first priority is to keep water out. RoofLink can help identify urgent openings, secure temporary protection, and document visible roof damage before permanent work begins.

Storm restoration may be a small repair, a larger replacement area, or a full roof replacement depending on material age, impact pattern, code requirements, and insurer review.

Storm response includes

  • Emergency roof review for missing shingles, exposed decking, punctures, and displaced flashing.
  • Temporary tarp installation or weatherproofing where active water entry is likely.
  • Photo documentation of hail bruising, wind creases, lifted tabs, accessory damage, and debris impact.
  • Repair scope preparation for affected slopes, ridges, vents, gutters, and flashing transitions.
  • Coordination-friendly notes that homeowners can use when speaking with insurance representatives.
  • Permanent repairs or replacement after the approved scope is understood.

What to do first

If water is entering the home, move valuables, document interior damage, and request help quickly. Avoid climbing onto a wet or damaged roof. A temporary cover can reduce additional damage while the restoration path is decided.

Best for

Hail impact, missing shingles, wind damage, active leaks after storms, emergency covers, and restoration planning.
