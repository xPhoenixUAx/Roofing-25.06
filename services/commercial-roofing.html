Commercial Roofing

Home/Services/Commercial Roofing
Low-slope roof care

Predictable roofing support for working buildings

Commercial roof issues interrupt tenants, customers, inventory, equipment, and operations. RoofLink helps commercial property contacts submit organized requests for inspections, leak tracing, membrane repairs, drainage review, and replacement planning.

The platform helps organize access notes, roof type, urgency, and maintenance context before provider follow-up.

Commercial services include

  • Low-slope roof inspections for membrane seams, punctures, ponding areas, terminations, and drains.
  • Leak tracing around rooftop units, vents, skylights, parapets, scuppers, and transitions.
  • Targeted membrane patching, flashing repair, sealant renewal, and accessory correction.
  • Drainage recommendations for ponding water, clogged drains, and poorly routed runoff.
  • Preventive maintenance plans with photo notes, priority repairs, and budget timing.
  • Replacement planning for small commercial roofs when repairs are no longer reliable.

For property managers

Service notes are written for decision-making: what failed, what was done, what still needs monitoring, and what timing should be considered for capital planning.

Best for

Offices, retail buildings, small warehouses, mixed-use properties, low-slope roofs, and managed maintenance programs.
