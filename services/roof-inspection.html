Know the condition

Inspection that turns uncertainty into a clear plan

A useful roof inspection does more than say whether a roof looks good from the ground. IronPeak reviews the surfaces and details that determine whether the system can keep shedding water through wind, snow, heat, and heavy rain.

Inspections are useful before buying a property, after severe weather, when stains appear indoors, before listing a home, or when planning maintenance for an aging roof.

Inspection checkpoints

  • Shingle condition, granule loss, curling, cracking, nail exposure, and fastening concerns.
  • Flashing at chimneys, walls, skylights, vents, valleys, and roof edges.
  • Pipe boots, roof vents, ridge ventilation, intake ventilation, and moisture-related warning signs.
  • Gutters, downspouts, fascia edges, and drainage paths that affect the roof perimeter.
  • Storm indicators such as bruising, missing tabs, wind creasing, displaced accessories, and impact marks.
  • Photo-supported findings with repair priorities and replacement timing when needed.

Inspection outcomes

After the visit, you receive a plain-language summary: what is performing, what should be watched, what needs repair, and what would justify replacement. The goal is to support a confident decision, not create pressure.

Best for

Home purchases, leak tracing, annual maintenance, storm review, insurance documentation, and replacement planning.
